ℹ️ How does indexing this website work?

You only add one page - your homepage - but the AI doesn't just learn from that page. Every time it indexes (right after you add a website, or whenever you hit Reindex), it first checks the site's sitemap.xml, which is the site's own list of its real pages, and is faster and more accurate than guessing from links. If there's no sitemap, it falls back to following links starting from the homepage instead.

Either way, it's capped at 25 pages so indexing stays fast and the AI's answers stay focused, and it automatically skips pages that aren't useful knowledge - login, cart, checkout, account, search, and admin pages, plus files like PDFs and images.
