    ℹ️ How Automation Rules work

    Every rule you create is checked against all of your customers automatically, once a minute, in the background - you don't need to open a conversation or check anything manually. When a customer matches every condition on an enabled rule at the same time, that rule's action runs for them right away.

    Right now the only action is Create Reminder: it adds a follow-up card (with the text and due date you set) to that customer's Follow-up Reminders and to the 🔔 Follow-ups page, so it's ready for you the moment it's due.

    🧩 What each Condition option means

    • Lead Score (0-100) - the AI's overall score for how sales-ready this lead is right now, combining their pipeline stage and the AI's confidence. Use ≥ to catch your hottest leads (e.g. Lead Score ≥ 80).
    • Lead Status - the deal stage shown in Lead Management: New, Interested, Qualified, Proposal Sent, Closed Won, or Closed Lost. Only equals (=) and does not equal (≠) make sense here, since it's a name, not a number.
    • Sentiment - how the customer is coming across in the conversation, per the AI: Positive, Neutral, or Negative. Also uses = / ≠.
    • Last Seen (Days) - how many days it's been since the customer's last message. Good for catching people who've gone quiet - e.g. Last Seen (Days) ≥ 5 to flag anyone silent for 5+ days.
    • AI Confidence (0-100) - how confident the AI is in its own read of this customer's intent and stage. A low value alongside other conditions can be a sign the AI's assessment needs a second look.

    Combining conditions narrows the match: a rule with both "Lead Score ≥ 80" and "Last Seen (Days) ≥ 3" only fires for customers who are both hot and have gone quiet for a few days - not for everyone who meets just one of those.
