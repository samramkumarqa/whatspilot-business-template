## Tier breakdown

### Starter — "AI Auto-Reply Setup"
**Price: $129 one-time** (delivery: 3 days)

What's included:
- Connect your existing WhatsApp Business number via Twilio
- AI trained on your business info (services, FAQs, pricing) to auto-reply to customer messages 24/7
- Every conversation automatically logged and searchable
- Basic lead capture — every customer who messages you becomes a tracked contact
- 1 round of AI response tuning after go-live

Add-on available: Website content ingestion — AI reads your website/docs to answer detailed
product questions (+$49)

### Standard — "AI Assistant + CRM" (recommended tier)
**Price: $299 one-time** (delivery: 5 days)

Everything in Starter, plus:
- Full CRM: customer profiles, lead status tracking, deal/opportunity pipeline
- Up to 5 automation rules (e.g. "tag as Hot Lead if customer asks about pricing," auto-create
  a follow-up reminder, notify you of high-value conversations)
- Follow-up reminders so no lead goes cold
- Human handoff — AI pauses automatically when a customer asks for a real person
- Manual reply option — you or your team can jump into any conversation from the dashboard

### Advanced — "Full Sales Assistant" 
**Price: $549 one-time** (delivery: 7 days)

Everything in Standard, plus:
- Analytics dashboard — revenue trend, lead conversion, automation rule performance
- AI lead scoring — automatically flags your highest-intent conversations
- Custom domain + hosting setup, so this is a page you own, not a shared tool
- 30 days of post-launch support included

**All tiers include:** a private login for your dashboard, your own WhatsApp number (not shared
with other businesses), and a walkthrough call before handoff.

## FAQ section (Upwork lets you add these to the listing)

**Q: Do I need any technical knowledge to use this?**
No. Once set up, you manage everything from a simple dashboard — no coding required.

**Q: Will this replace my existing WhatsApp Business app?**
No — it connects to your WhatsApp Business number so the AI can respond automatically, but you
keep full visibility and can jump in and reply personally any time.

**Q: What if the AI gives a wrong answer?**
You can review and correct AI responses, and set up automatic handoff so the AI steps back the
moment a customer asks for a human.

**Q: Is my customer data kept private?**
Yes — your WhatsApp number, customer data, and conversations are isolated to your account only.

**Q: What happens after the initial setup?**
You own a working system from day one. Ongoing hosting/support is available separately if you
want me to keep maintaining and improving it.
